Description of Services

Kart Connect is a data-driven setup management platform designed to help drivers, mechanics, and team managers record, analyse, and optimise kart setup across sessions and race weekends. The Platform provides the following features:

  • —
    Session Logging — detailed recording of setup changes, tyre data, weather conditions, and driver feedback for every on-track session.
  • —
    AI Setup Advisor — voice and text-enabled tool returning three prioritised setup adjustments with full technical explanations.
  • —
    PDF Comparison Reports — exportable session-by-session comparison reports presenting setup data and performance trends.
  • —
    QR Team Comparison — Team Manager tool enabling QR-code-based setup sharing across drivers in the same class. Guest access requires no account and expires after four days.
  • —
    Tyre Pressure Stagger Tool — calculates recommended starting tyre pressures based on previous session data.
  • —
    Dashboard Scanner — post-session data capture for engine and tyre performance analysis.
  • —
    Met Office Track Integration — automatic logging of real-time weather data against each session record.
  • —
    Shared Baseline Library — team-accessible storage of proven setup baselines.
  • —
    White-Label Setup Sheets — Pro Team users may export fully branded setup sheets with team logo and colours.
  • —
    Advanced Team Analytics — comprehensive performance data across an entire driver roster (Pro Team only).

Features available to each User are determined by their active subscription tier. We reserve the right to modify, add, or remove features with reasonable notice.
