KART CONNECT TRI-FOLD LEAFLET: NOTES FOR THE DESIGNER / PRINTER

FORMAT
- A4 landscape (297 x 210 mm), printed double-sided, roll-folded (letter fold) into thirds.
  Folded size is roughly 100 x 210 mm (DL-ish).
- Page 1 of the PDF = OUTSIDE (dark | light | dark).  Page 2 = INSIDE (light | dark | light).
- Panel widths, left to right:
    OUTSIDE: fold-in flap 97 mm | back cover 100 mm | front cover 100 mm
    INSIDE:  inside left 100 mm | inside middle 100 mm | inside right (fold-in) 97 mm
  The fold-in panel is 3 mm narrower so it tucks in flat.
  Fold lines: outside at 97 mm and 197 mm from the left; inside at 100 mm and 200 mm from the left.
- Print file: kart-connect-trifold-A4-print-3mm-bleed.pdf
  Page size 303 x 216 mm = A4 plus 3 mm bleed on every side. All text sits at least 7 mm inside the trim and fold lines.
- Fold guide file: kart-connect-trifold-A4-fold-guides.pdf
  Trimmed A4 with pink dashed FOLD lines, for checking only. Do not print it.
- No crop or fold marks are on the print file. Please add them if the printer needs them.
- The PDF is RGB. Convert to CMYK and check the yellow-green gradients.
- Suggested stock: 170 gsm silk (folds cleanly). Crease before folding if heavier.
