## 1. Add weather (3 slides)
Simple. There's an **Add weather** button in the app: tap it and the conditions are filled in for you
(air temperature, humidity, wind, conditions). No looking anything up after the session.
Say "live weather data". Do not say "Met Office".

## 2. Scan your dashboard (4 slides)
The advantages of scanning the data logger dashboard after a session instead of typing the numbers in by hand.
The scan reads best lap, max and min RPM, engine and exhaust temperatures, and top speed from a photo of the dash.
Advantages: quicker, no mistyped numbers, done before you forget, no copying figures off a small screen.

## 3. No signal? No problem (4 slides)
Losing signal at the track is annoying and stops data going in. With Kart Connect you open the app the day before
so everything is downloaded, keep working offline, and it syncs when you're back on signal.
Merge in the QR codes: the team manager sends each mechanic a QR code, the mechanic scans it and submits the setup data,
and it goes back to the team manager. Works offline too and syncs as soon as there's a connection, at the track or back at the hotel.

## 4. Set up your profile once (4 slides)
The user enters their details once on their profile: karts, chassis numbers, engine numbers, carbs, exhausts.
When they start a session, all of it fills into that session automatically.

## 5. Voice notes (3–4 slides)
Speak session notes instead of typing them (gloves on, hands busy, between heats). Notes are kept with the session.

## 6. Leaderboard (4 slides)
Team and Pro Team leaderboard of lap times across drivers. Every lap time has a preview of the session's setup PDF next to it,
so you can see the setup used to set that time. Example story: five drivers, and one driver appears three times,
so you can open each of their three setups. Use invented driver names or "Driver 1–5", never real people.

## 7. Race weekend timeline (3 slides)
Simple. Every session of a race weekend kept together, in order, from practice to the final.

## 8. Units, your way (3 slides)
Simple. Change units to suit you: pressure (bar or psi), temperature (°C or °F), speed (km/h or mph),
altitude (metres or feet), and more. If `assets/shots/units.png` exists, use it as the real screenshot.

## 9. In your language (3 slides)
Short and simple. The app is available in eight languages: English, Spanish, French, Italian, Arabic,
German, Portuguese and Japanese. Show the language names in their own script (English, Español, Français,
Italiano, العربية, Deutsch, Português, 日本語). No flags or emoji.
